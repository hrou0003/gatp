# Lecture 05: Problems

1. Show that (ℝ, 𝒪_std) is Hausdorff but (ℝ, {∅, ℝ}) is not.

2. Prove that the discrete topology on any set M is Hausdorff.

3. Find an open cover of (0, 1) with no finite subcover. Conclude (0, 1) is not compact.

  {(1/n, 1 − 1/n) | n ≥ 3}. The intervals are nested: (1/3, 2/3) ⊂ (1/4, 3/4) ⊂ ... Union of all is (0, 1). No finite subcover: the union of finitely many is the largest interval, which misses points near 0 and 1.

4. Prove that [0, 1] is compact using the Heine-Borel theorem.

4. Let S = {(x, sin(1/x)) | x ∈ (0, 1]} ∪ {(0, 0)} ⊂ ℝ². Argue that S is connected but not path-connected.

5. Show that ℝ \ {0} is not connected.

6. Compute the fundamental group of the cylinder C = ℝ × S¹.

7. Prove that path-connected implies connected.

8. Let φ: M → N be a homeomorphism and π₁(p) the fundamental group at p ∈ M. How does π₁(φ(p)) relate to π₁(p)?
