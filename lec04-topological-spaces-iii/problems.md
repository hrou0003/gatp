# Lecture 04: Problems

1. Let M = {a, b, c}. Verify that 𝒪 = {∅, {a}, {a, b}, M} is a topology on M.

2. In the topology 𝒪 = {∅, {a}, {a, b}, M} on {a, b, c}, classify each subset as open, closed, both, or neither.

3. Let q(n) = 1/(n+1) in ℝ. Does q converge in (a) discrete topology, (b) chaotic topology, (c) standard topology?

4. Prove that φ: (ℝ, 𝒪_std) → (ℝ, 𝒪_std), φ(x) = x² is continuous.

5. Show that (0, 1) and ℝ are homeomorphic (standard topologies).

6. Let N ⊂ M and 𝒪|_N = {U ∩ N | U ∈ 𝒪} be the induced topology. Prove that 𝒪|_N is a topology on N.

7. Let ∼ on ℝ be x ~ y :⇔ x − y ∈ 2πℤ. Describe the quotient topology on ℝ/∼. What does an open set in ℝ/∼ look like?

8. Let (A, 𝒪_A) and (B, 𝒪_B) be topological spaces. Prove that the product topology 𝒪_{A×B} (generated by {S × T | S ∈ 𝒪_A, T ∈ 𝒪_B}) is a topology on A × B.
